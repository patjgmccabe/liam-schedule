How to Make a Pizza

Tap each step when you finish it.

  1. 1

    Turn the oven on.

    Set it to 475°F.
  2. 2

    Put the crust on the pan.

    Lay it flat in the middle.
  3. 3

    Pour on the sauce.

    Spread it around in circles.
  4. 4

    Pour on the cheese.

    Use the whole bag.
  5. 5

    Put the pepperoni on top.

    Spread them out evenly.
  6. 6

    Bake the pizza.

    When the oven is ready, put it in for 7 minutes.
Stay safe: The oven is very hot. Wear oven mitts, and ask a helper for the hot oven parts.

Tip: tap a step to check it off. Print this page to keep it in the kitchen.
